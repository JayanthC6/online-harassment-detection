# ShieldAI — User & Client Guide

Welcome to **ShieldAI**, an intelligent cyber safety and harassment defense platform designed to protect individuals, communities, and organizations from digital abuse, extortion, and fraud.

---

## 🌟 What is ShieldAI?

ShieldAI acts as a digital protective shield against online threats. It automatically analyzes suspicious messages, social media posts, chat exports, and screenshots to detect:
- **Online Harassment & Cyberbullying:** Targeted insults, hate speech, and persistent intimidation.
- **Physical Threats & Stalking:** Violence threats, doxxing (releasing private info), and real-world harm.
- **Digital Extortion & Sextortion:** Intimidation schemes demanding money or cryptocurrency under threat of leaking private photos or webcam footage.
- **Financial Phishing & Scams:** Fake banking messages (e.g. KYC updates, electricity disconnection notices, job offers) designed to steal passwords or money.
- **AI Cyber Safety Assistant:** A dedicated advisor providing empathetic, step-by-step safety guidance for victims and structured investigation reports for security reviewers.

## 🚀 Getting Started in 3 Steps

No complex setup is required. The platform comes pre-configured with ready-to-run models and demo data.

### Step 1: Start the Application
- **Windows:** Double-click `start.bat` (or open PowerShell and run `.\start.ps1`).
- This launches both the backend analysis engine (Port 5000) and the web dashboard (Port 5173).

### Step 2: Open Your Web Browser
- Visit: **[http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173)**
- You will see the ShieldAI Security Operations Center and navigation menu.

### Step 3: Run Your First Scan
1. Click **"Threat Hunt & Scan"** on the left menu.
2. Paste any message or upload a screenshot.
3. Click **"Analyze Message"** to receive an instant risk assessment, breakdown of detected signals, and recommended actions.

## 🛡️ Key Features & How to Use Them

### 1. Dual-Persona Safety Assistant
Click **"AI Assistant"** on the navigation menu:
- **Victim Support Mode:** Designed for someone facing harassment or extortion. The assistant responds empathetically, never blames the user, and gives up to 6 clear, actionable steps (e.g. how to preserve evidence, block the actor, secure accounts, and report to authorities).
- **Analyst Mode:** Designed for moderators, school administrators, or security teams. It formats the output into a technical incident assessment with verified indicators, observed attacker tactics, and legal guidance under the Information Technology Act.
- **Engine Transparency:** A badge at the bottom of each reply shows whether the answer was generated by `Groq`, `Claude`, or the deterministic `Rules` engine.

### 2. Privacy-First Protection & Private Scan Mode
- **Automatic Privacy Masking:** All sensitive personal data (phone numbers, email addresses, national IDs like Aadhaar and PAN cards) is masked with redaction tags before analysis or storage.
- **Private Scan Option:** When scanning sensitive communications, simply check the **"Private Scan"** box on the Scan page. Your text will be analyzed entirely in memory and will **never** be saved to the database or visible to moderators.

### 3. Reviewer Dashboard & Repeat Offender Tracking
- **Moderator Queue:** Security reviewers can triage flagged messages by risk tier (Low, Medium, High, Critical), search by keyword, and update resolution statuses.
- **Repeat Offenders:** Automatically groups incidents by phone number, username, domain, or crypto wallet to highlight chronic harassers across platforms.
- **Conversations & Chat Exports:** Upload WhatsApp (`.txt`) or Telegram (`.json`) chat logs to automatically evaluate conversations and isolate the aggressor from defenders.

### 4. Real-Time Browser Extension
- Located in the `extension/` folder. Load it into Google Chrome (`chrome://extensions/` with Developer Mode enabled) to automatically flag toxic or deceptive comments on live websites.

## 💡 Quick Troubleshooting & FAQ

- **The web app says "Backend Offline":** Ensure the terminal running `start.bat` is still open. Verify that port 5000 is available.
- **Does ShieldAI require internet to work?** No. The core DistilBERT machine learning model, rule heuristics, offline knowledge base, and database run completely offline on your local computer. External AI features (Groq or Claude) are optional.
- **How do I reset demo data?** Run `python scripts/reset_demo.py` in your terminal to refresh the database to a clean, canonical state.
